About Us

Toyama Express Japanese Restaurant brings the authentic flavors of Japan to the heart of Charlotte, NC. Nestled in the Arbors neighborhood, this beloved local spot has earned the trust of over a thousand guests who keep coming back for its fresh, satisfying Japanese cuisine served with genuine hospitality.

With a menu rooted in time-honored Japanese culinary tradition, Toyama Express is committed to quality ingredients and bold, clean flavors. Whether guests are craving a quick lunch or a relaxed dinner, the kitchen delivers a consistently rewarding dining experience.

Conveniently located at 2130 E Arbors Dr in Charlotte, Toyama Express is a neighborhood favorite that welcomes families, coworkers, and food lovers alike. The warm atmosphere and attentive service make every visit feel like a special occasion.
