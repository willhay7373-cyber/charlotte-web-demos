Our Story

A Charlotte Institution

4.7 out of 5 - 1,024 happy guests

Chuck's Grill has been a cornerstone of the Charlotte community, serving up bold American flavors that keep neighbors coming back again and again. Nestled in north Charlotte, this beloved neighborhood grill has earned the trust and loyalty of over a thousand local regulars - and a near-perfect 4.7-star rating to prove it.

From juicy hand-pressed burgers to slow-smoked ribs that fall right off the bone, Chuck's Grill is where comfort food gets the respect it deserves. The kitchen takes pride in doing the classics right - fresh ingredients, generous portions, and the kind of honest cooking that reminds you why homegrown spots beat the chains every time.

Whether you're stopping in for a weeknight dinner, cheering on your team with a cold beer and a plate of wings, or treating the family to a Saturday feast, Chuck's Grill delivers the warmth and flavor that Charlotte locals have come to love. This isn't just a restaurant - it's your neighborhood table.
